The Heart of Fancy Fragrances

A true South African family effort born out of passion, hard work, and family love.

Our Origin Story

Fancy Fragrances started in 2024 around a family kitchen table. Founder Tsholofelo ("Tsholo") Kabasia built the business alongside her three children, naming the brand after the affectionate nickname she has called her youngest daughter since birth.

At its heart, Fancy Fragrances is a family effort dedicated to providing good-quality perfumes and skincare products at prices every South African family can afford.

Hands-On Quality

Tsholo runs operations with care—hand-packing orders, formulating blends, and meeting regular buyers face-to-face at local weekend markets. Having established a loyal customer base of over 200 buyers, this web platform allows us to take orders day and night so every customer can easily reorder their favorite scents from home.
