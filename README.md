## Project Overview: Fancy Fragrances
Fancy Fragrances is a local South African family business established in 2024 by Tsholofelo ("Tsholo") Kabasia and her children. The business produces and sells high-quality, handcrafted perfumes and skincare products at affordable rates. 

This web application automates customer inquiries, showcases the 10-item core product line, facilitates online reorders via stall QR codes, and provides a dedicated damage reporting system for fast replacements.

---

## Key Site Features & Pages Implemented
1. **`index.html` (Homepage):** Top announcement bar, main hero showcase, brand origin preview, featured products grid, next market locator, and customer reviews.
2. **`shop.html` (Catalogue Page):** Detailed listing of all 10 perfume and skincare items with key ingredients, scent notes, pricing, and category filters.
3. **`about.html` (Brand Story):** Full historical narrative detailing the family origin story from kitchen table to market stalls.
4. **`report-damage.html` (Damaged Item Form):** Specialized reporting interface allowing buyers to submit order details and photo proof for quick resolution within 24–48 hours.
5. **`contact.html` (Contact & Locator):** Click-to-call, direct WhatsApp integration, general email contact form, and market schedule.

## About the website

Fancy Fragrances is a small South African family fragrance and skincare business. The website gives customers a simple way to learn about the brand, browse the catalogue, contact the business, and find upcoming market events.

## Pages

- `home.html` - welcome page, featured products, reviews, and market preview
- `shop.html` - skincare catalogue and perfume launch notice
- `about.html` - the Fancy Fragrances family story
- `contact.html` - contact links and enquiry form
- `damaged-item.html` - damaged product report form
- `markets.html` - upcoming market information

## Technologies

- HTML5 for page structure and accessible form labels
- CSS3 in `css/home.css` for the shared visual design and responsive breakpoints
- Local image assets in `pics/`

## Running the project

Open `home.html` in a browser, or use the Live Server extension in VS Code. No build process or package installation is required.

## Responsive design

The layout has desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints. The navigation wraps on smaller screens, product cards resize into fewer columns, forms use the available screen width, and images keep their proportions.

## Part 2 improvements

The Part 2 update includes a more consistent colour palette and typography system, hover and focus states, responsive layout adjustments, improved form styling, new skincare product photography, and a clear notice that perfumes will be added soon. The website uses HTML and CSS only.
